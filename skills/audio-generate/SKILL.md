# Audio Generate

## Overview
Define the audio layer for short drama or news videos: voice, music, and sound effects.

## Workflow

1. **Voice plan**
   - Map each scene/shot to a voice line and speaker.
   - Keep lines short and aligned with clip durations.

2. **Music mood**
   - Specify mood per segment and when to fade in/out.

3. **SFX cues**
   - Add minimal, high-impact cues (steps, door, ambient).

4. **Delivery notes**
   - Loudness target, language, and pacing.

## Output Guidance

- Keep audio notes aligned to shot IDs.
- Avoid over-layering; prioritize clarity of speech.
- Use consistent speaker names for TTS integration.
