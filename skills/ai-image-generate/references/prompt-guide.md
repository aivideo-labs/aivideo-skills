# Prompt guide (image)

Keep prompts concrete and scene-based. Include subject, setting, lighting, lens, and mood.

## Suggested structure

- Subject + action
- Environment + time of day
- Lighting + camera
- Style tags (optional)
- Constraints (optional)

## Examples

- "a cinematic portrait of a chef in a stainless-steel kitchen, soft rim light, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field"
- "minimalist product shot of a smartwatch on a marble slab, diffused studio lighting, clean background"

## Negative prompts

Use short, specific negatives when quality issues appear:
- "blurry, low quality, watermark, distorted hands"
