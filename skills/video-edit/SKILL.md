# Video Edit

## Overview
Define how generated clips are assembled into a final short video.

## Workflow

1. **Collect assets**
   - Ensure all shot clips exist and are named consistently.

2. **Assemble timeline**
   - Order clips by story or fact flow.
   - Keep pacing tight; avoid long static shots.

3. **Transitions and titles**
   - Use minimal transitions; prioritize hard cuts.
   - Add title card and end credits if needed.

4. **Subtitles**
   - Provide concise lines tied to shot IDs.

5. **Export**
   - Define format, resolution, aspect ratio, and runtime.

## Output Guidance

- Use the templates in references for edit-plan and final-delivery.
- Keep export settings consistent across series.
