# Prompt guide (video)

Describe motion explicitly. Include camera movement, subject motion, and scene dynamics.

## Suggested structure

- Subject + action
- Scene + environment
- Camera motion
- Lighting + mood
- Constraints (optional)

## Examples

- "a wide drone shot over a coastal road at golden hour, slow forward motion, gentle parallax"
- "close-up of a cyclist pedaling through rain, handheld camera, reflections on wet asphalt"

## Tips

- Keep durations realistic for the chosen provider.
- Use shorter prompts for better adherence in some backends.
